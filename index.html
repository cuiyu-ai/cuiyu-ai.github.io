About Me

I am Yu Cui, a humorous AI researcher, advised by Haibin Zhang and Sisi Duan. My research interests include LLM Inference Optimization, Agent Harness, and AI Safety.

I also lead a 15-member research team, with members primarily from Tsinghua University, Beijing Institute of Technology, University of Chinese Academy of Sciences, and Beijing University of Technology. We seek to explore and pursue research that creates practical value and meaningful impact in the real world, particularly in industry.

If our research resonates with you, we welcome you to join us.
